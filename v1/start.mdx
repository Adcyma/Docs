## Welcome to Adcyma's Documentation

Adcyma is an **Identity Governance and Administration (IGA)** platform built for **Microsoft Entra ID** and **Active Directory**. It automates identity lifecycle management, from onboarding to offboarding, so you can stop doing it by hand.

This documentation covers setup, configuration, and day-to-day use. Use the sidebar to navigate, or the search bar to find what you need.

## What Adcyma does

- Automates user onboarding, role changes, and offboarding through configurable workflows
- Replaces manual, error-prone processes with consistent automation
- Provides role-based access controls and detailed audit trails
- Lets employees request access to resources through the self-service Access Center, with configurable approval flows
- Includes compliance tools: access inventory, hygiene checks for stale groups and accounts, and audit-ready reports for SOC 2, ISO 27001, and NIS2
- Works with cloud-only Entra ID, on-premises Active Directory (via a lightweight agent), or hybrid environments
- Runs as a SaaS product with no on-premises infrastructure required beyond the optional AD agent
- Works with non-technical administrators through a straightforward interface
- Costs roughly 70% less than traditional ILM solutions

## The Two Main Areas

**People Hub** is where the employee lifecycle is managed: onboarding new hires, updating existing employees, and offboarding leavers, with a live view of queued automation tasks and history. See [Using Your People Hub](./peoplehub).

**Access Center** is the self-service identity governance portal: employees request access to resources, approvers act on requests, and resource owners manage their resources. It's part of the Identity Governance add-on. See [Access Center](./administration/Access%20Governance/accesscenter).

Administrators additionally get the **Administration** area, covered section by section in these docs.

## Key Terminology

These core concepts come up throughout the docs.

### Workflows
A [Workflow](./administration/Lifecycle%20Management/workflows) is a configurable sequence of logic that automates identity management processes. Every workflow has three parts:

- A **trigger** is the event that starts the workflow (e.g., new hire, role change, termination)
- A **condition** is business logic that determines when actions fire (e.g., department = "Sales", location = "Stockholm")
- An **action** is what happens when conditions are met (e.g., add to groups, assign role templates, send notifications)

*Example*: A new employee workflow triggers when someone joins the Sales department, checks their location and role, then adds them to the right groups for sales tools, regional resources, and company-wide access.

### Role Templates
[Role Templates](./administration/Lifecycle%20Management/roletemplate) bundle related directory groups into a single assignable unit. Instead of manually assigning a dozen group memberships one by one, you assign one Role Template through a Workflow and the user gets everything they need.

*Example*: A "Finance Team" role template might include groups for:
- Financial reporting tools
- Expense management systems
- Department-specific SharePoint sites
- Budget planning applications
- Compliance and audit resources

When a new finance employee is hired, a single workflow action assigns the entire Role Template. No manual steps, no forgotten permissions.

### Resources
A [Resource](./administration/Access%20Governance/resourcemanagement) is something employees can request access to through the Access Center: an application, a shared drive, a physical location. Each resource maps to one or more directory groups and is governed by an [Approval Flow](./administration/Access%20Governance/approvalflows).
