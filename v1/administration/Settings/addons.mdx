# Add-ons

import Image from '@theme/IdealImage';
import addOnsImg from '@site/static/img/AddOns.jpg';

The Add-ons page is where you extend your platform with modules and connect the tools your team uses. Add-ons are grouped into what's **Active** on your subscription, what's **Available** to enable, and integrations that are **Coming Soon**.

<Image img={addOnsImg} alt="Add-ons" className="doc-screenshot" />

## Identity Governance (IGA)

The Identity Governance module is a paid add-on that unlocks the Access Governance features: access certifications, approval workflows, entitlement management, the Access Center, and compliance reporting.

Click **Enable** to add it to your subscription. It's billed at the same interval and seat count as your base plan. You can disable it again at any time; disabling stops the features and any unused time is credited on your next invoice.

:::info
If you're on the free tier, paid add-ons show **Upgrade to enable** and take you to [Billing](./billing) first.
:::

## Coming Soon

Upcoming integrations are listed by category so you can see what's on the roadmap:

- **HR Systems** - BambooHR, Personio, HiBob (sync employee lifecycle events automatically)
- **Telecom** - Telia, Tele2, Telavox (mobile subscription provisioning)
- **Collaboration** - Slack, GitHub, Microsoft Teams (team, channel, and repository access management)

If one of these would be valuable to your organization, let us know at hello@adcyma.com; interest helps us prioritize.
